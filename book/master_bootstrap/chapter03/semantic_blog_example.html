Understanding Semantic HTML

Published on June 10, 2024 by John Doe

Semantic HTML is the use of HTML markup to reinforce the meaning of the information in webpages rather than merely to define its presentation. It helps both browsers and developers understand the structure and content of a webpage more effectively.
